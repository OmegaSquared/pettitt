About PETTITT WEALTH

Personal Financial Planning. Thoughtful Analysis. Genuine Relationships.

At PETTITT WEALTH

Financial Planning Built Around You

At PETTITT WEALTH, financial planning starts with understanding the person behind the numbers.

We work with entrepreneurs, business leaders, high-net-worth individuals, and people navigating important life transitions. Our approach combines detailed financial analysis with personal attention, helping clients evaluate their options in the context of their individual goals and circumstances.

The philosophy is straightforward: take the time to understand the situation, dig into the details, and build a strategy around what matters most.

Our Values

The Values Behind the Relationship

Fiduciary Integrity

Approach financial advice with integrity, transparency, and attention to each client’s circumstances and objectives.

Intentional Focus

Work with a select group of clients to support meaningful personal attention and direct communication.

Transparency & Clarity

Make financial conversations approachable and recommendations understandable, so clients can ask questions and participate in decisions.

Comprehensive Planning

Consider how investments, retirement, business finances, insurance, and estate considerations fit together rather than viewing each decision in isolation.

Personal Relationships

Build long-term relationships through listening, honest conversations, and an understanding of what matters to each client.

A Financial Planning Relationship Built Around You

PETTITT WEALTH is built on the belief that clients deserve to be understood as individuals.

That means taking the time to ask questions, examine the details, discuss options, and consider how financial decisions connect to the bigger picture.

When appropriate, Eric collaborates with accountants and estate-planning attorneys to help coordinate the professionals involved in a client’s financial life.

Every client is different. Every financial plan should be personal.
